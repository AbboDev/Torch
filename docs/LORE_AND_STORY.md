# Lore & Story Documentation

## 1. Executive Summary & Premise

_Torch_ is set in a sci-fi universe where humanity has achieved space exploration and relative galactic peace. The protagonist is an ambitious space marine dreaming of joining the elite guard. However, due to a prolonged era of peace, opportunities for active duty are virtually non-existent.

When a distress signal arrives from a remote, unmapped alien planet being studied by the Space Federation, the protagonist's squad is deployed to investigate. Upon landing, a catastrophic ambush by an overwhelming horde of dark entities exterminates the squad. The protagonist flees into a ruined ancient temple, absorbing an ancient relic known as **Torch**, which provides a vital source of illumination and energy capable of repelling the dark creatures. A sudden floor collapse plunges the hero into an abyss, resulting in the loss of their base human equipment. From this moment, the journey of survival, rediscovery, and cosmic confrontation begins.

---

## 2. The Four Narrative Acts

### Act I: The Simulation & The Fall (Tutorial)

- **Context:** Aboard the Federation starship, the protagonist runs through a high-tech combat and movement simulator to prove their readiness for the elite guard.
- **Gameplay & Narrative Integration:** This serves as the full-power tutorial. The player experiences the maximum potential of the suit (double jumps, dash, advanced weapons, powerful illumination) before reality strikes. Following the simulation, the real mission unfolds on the remote alien planet, ending in tragedy at the ancient temple where the _Torch_ relic is absorbed and gear is lost.

### Act II: The First Adventure & The Renegade Clan

- **Context:** Trapped deep underground, the hero must ascend through low-visibility caverns, managing environmental hazards (toxic gas, extreme temperatures) and utilizing basic light modules.
- **The Threat:** Exploration reveals that the dark corruption is not merely a natural phenomenon, but the result of prohibited experiments conducted by a **renegade alien clan** banished by the primary subterranean civilization.
- **Climax of Act II:** Guided implicitly by the surviving peaceful alien race, the hero infiltrates the clan's fortified underground laboratories, defeats their leadership, triggers a timed self-destruction sequence, and escapes via a salvage shuttle carrying biological sample containers back to the Federation.

### Act III: Return to Earth & The Containment Breach

- **Context:** The hero returns to the mother planet (Earth) to deliver reports and samples to the Space Federation.
- **The Catastrophe:** While samples are analyzed in a high-security research facility, a rapid containment breach occurs due to covert infiltration. The dark threat infects the urban/laboratory complex.
- **The Escape:** The hero, caught off guard and unable to fight off overwhelming forces alone in an urban setting, acquires minor upgrades and flees through maintenance tunnels and sewers. Realizing the Federation is in disarray, the hero takes matters into their own hands, securing an emergency reconnaissance shuttle for an unauthorized return to the alien world.

### Act IV: The Second Adventure & The Final Climax

- **Context:** The hero returns to the alien planet in triumph and full power, blending advanced Federation technology with mastered alien relic capabilities.
- **The Resolution:** With the backing of the rising native alien population, the hero plunges into the deepest core of the planet to eradicate the true origin of the darkness once and for all, achieving redemption and saving both worlds.

---

## 3. Lore & Thematic Elements

- **Light as a Weapon and Shield:** The _Torch_ relic is not just a flashlight; it represents an ancient, sentient energy that opposes the parasitic nature of the dark horde.
- **Federation vs. Ancient Civilization:** The contrast between rigid human military tech and organic, luminous alien relics defines the weapon upgrade path throughout the game.
